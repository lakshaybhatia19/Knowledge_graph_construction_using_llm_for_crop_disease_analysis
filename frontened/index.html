About The Project

Our system aims to combine image-based crop disease detection with structured knowledge representation and LLM-based analysis.

🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop an intelligent crop disease analysis system using the PlantVillage dataset. A deep learning model can be used to identify crop diseases from leaf images. The identified disease information can then be represented through a knowledge graph and analyzed using a Large Language Model.

🌿 PlantVillage Dataset

  • 📷 Crop leaf images
  • 🌱 Multiple crop categories
  • 🦠 Healthy and diseased leaves
  • 🧠 Used for disease classification
  • 🔗 Disease information can support graph construction

🦠 Disease

Tomato Late Blight


A disease that can affect tomato leaves, stems and fruits.

⚠️ Symptoms

  • Dark spots on leaves
  • Leaf discoloration
  • Rapid leaf damage
  • Fruit lesions

🔬 Possible Causes

  • Pathogen infection
  • High humidity
  • Wet environmental conditions
  • Poor air circulation

🛡️ Prevention

  • Monitor plants regularly
  • Maintain suitable spacing
  • Avoid excessive moisture
  • Remove severely infected material

💊 Treatment / Management

  • Remove infected parts
  • Improve air circulation
  • Follow suitable disease management
  • Follow agricultural recommendations

🎯 Project Summary

Problem

Crop diseases can negatively affect crop productivity. Identifying diseases and accessing relevant information can be difficult for users.

Proposed Solution

The proposed system combines crop disease classification, knowledge graph construction and Large Language Models for intelligent crop disease analysis.

Input

Crop leaf image from the PlantVillage dataset or user-provided image.

Output

Predicted disease, related knowledge, symptoms, causes, treatment information and LLM-generated explanation.
